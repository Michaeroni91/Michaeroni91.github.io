Tip 1: Get the Fastlane or Fastlane PLUS+

This goes a long way but it's also an expensive strategy. Typical Fastlane on Saturdays costs $150 while Fastlane PLUS+ costs a little over $200. Fastlane PLUS+ includes rides such as Valravn, Millennium Force, Steel Vengeance, Maverick, and Top Thrill 2. I used Fastlane PLUS during both of my trips to Canadas Wonderland which generates crowds on par with Cedar Point and that allowed me to get multiple rerides that I probably wouldn't have gotten without it. Despite the pricing, it's a very helpful strategy that allows you to minimize your wait times.

Tip 2: Start towards the back of the park first

This strategy is crucial if you end up not purchasing Fastlane PLUS+. You might be tempted to go to Gatekeeper or Raptor first, but beware that a lot of people will head to those rides first when the park opens. While Gatekeeper and Raptor are popular rides, they don't generate the peak wait times that rides in the back like Millennium Force, Maverick, and Steel Vengeance do. Millennium Force was the first ride I went to during my 2023 trip and I waited about 45 minutes for it, partially because many of those who stayed at the actual resort get to enter the park an hour early which I didn't stay at. Around 3:30, that line had increased to 2 hours, while Gatekeepers line at the front was only around 50 minutes.

Tip 3: Take advantage of ride downtime

During my trip to Cedar Point, there were several major rides that experienced temporary downtime. The benefit of this is that several people will leave the line to ride something else which will actually decrease the total wait time. I told myself I was gonna go back to Maverick later in the day but when I saw how long the Millennium Force line had gotten around 3:30, I became nervous of how long I would have to wait for Maverick. However, because the ride experienced a temporary delay in operation when I got in line, several people were starting to exit the line. This turned what probably would have a 75 minute wait into a 50 minute wait. The same thing happened to Raptor later in the day when I was 10 minutes into a near 2 hour wait for Valravn. I decided to leave the Valravn line and I ended up getting on Raptor in about 15 minutes.
