About Us

This is Michael Chen and Daniel Martinez, and we are the co-owners of Ezo Kitchen & Sushi Bar. The Sushi Bar is the headline attraction in a range of cuisines for visitors. We ensure the usage of authentic ingredients in all of our meals. Michael spent four years receiving training from master Sushi chefs in Tokyo and Los Angeles. The Ezo aspect of the name represents the former name of Hokkaido in Japan, which is where Michael’s grandparents emigrated from. We also believe that Vietnamese and Thai cuisines are underrepresented in the United States. After studying culinary traditions in Vietnam, Thailand, and Japan for 5 years, Daniel aspired to bring a greater selection of Asian dining options, expanding beyond Chinese takeout and hibachi. With the growing Asian-American population in the suburb, we aimed to bring our vision to life.
